# Zuliov — A Social Network for Social Impact

**Connecting nonprofit organizations, communities, and collaborators through project stories, transparency, and meaningful partnerships.**

🌐 **Live prototype:** [zuliov.vercel.app](https://zuliov.vercel.app/)  
💻 **Source code:** [github.com/FryDD0922/zuliov](https://github.com/FryDD0922/zuliov)

## About Zuliov

Zuliov is an early-stage social impact platform designed to help people discover nonprofit organizations, follow social projects, connect with communities, and explore opportunities for collaboration.

Many nonprofits and grassroots communities carry out meaningful work, yet their activities, progress, and needs can be difficult for potential supporters and partner organizations to discover. Zuliov aims to bring project updates, community participation, transparency, and collaboration together in one accessible web platform.

The long-term vision is to build a social network for impact where stories create connections, connections lead to partnerships, and partnerships can help communities take meaningful action.

## The Problem

Zuliov explores three interconnected challenges:

- **Visibility and transparency:** People need accessible information about organizations, their activities, and project progress.
- **Community connection:** Volunteers, supporters, and organizations need ways to discover and engage with communities working toward shared causes.
- **Collaboration:** Communities with specific needs may struggle to find organizations that can provide resources, expertise, or other forms of support.

Zuliov aims to help address these challenges through a combination of project information, community groups, and a proposed partnership workflow.

## Current Prototype Features

The current prototype includes interface concepts and interactive elements for exploring the following capabilities.

### 1. Nonprofit Profiles

The prototype includes an NGO profile concept with sections for:

- Organizational information and mission.
- Leadership and team information.
- Projects and project updates.
- Transparency-related information and impact metrics.
- Community feedback and contact information.

**Note:** Sample organizations, ratings, scores, and impact metrics displayed in the prototype are illustrative and should not be interpreted as verified real-world information.

### 2. Community Groups

Zuliov includes a group interface designed around causes, communities, and projects.

The prototype presents concepts for:

- Creating and exploring groups.
- Joining groups.
- Sharing updates and stories.
- Commenting on and sharing posts.
- Reporting potentially inappropriate content.
- Linking to external community channels, such as WhatsApp, Discord, and Slack.

The long-term vision includes both user-created community groups and official organization groups.

### 3. User Roles and Organization Participation

The product concept distinguishes between people participating in an organization and the permissions they have on the platform.

Potential organizational roles include members, volunteers, coordinators, and leaders. Platform permissions could determine who can publish directly, approve submitted content, or manage a group.

Reliable role verification and production-ready permission controls remain important areas for development.

### 4. Project Transparency

The prototype includes interface concepts for project updates, field activity, associated costs, funding status, audit information, and supporting evidence.

The goal is to help organizations document project progress over time and make relevant information more accessible to supporters.

Zuliov intends to distinguish between information published by an organization, information reviewed by others, and outcomes that have been independently verified.

### 5. Partnerships and Collaboration — Future Vision

An important part of Zuliov's planned development is a structured partnership system.

For example, a community could submit a request to an NGO explaining a specific need, the proposed objective, available supporting documents, and the type of assistance sought.

The receiving organization could review the request, ask for additional information, and decide whether to collaborate. Accepted partnerships could establish shared objectives, responsibilities, updates, and evidence of progress.

This workflow is part of the broader product vision and is not presented as a fully operational partnership management system.

## Current Development Status

Zuliov is an early-stage prototype, not yet a complete production platform.

The current repository contains a web interface implemented in a single HTML file, with client-side styling and JavaScript interactions.

Some interactions and interface states are demonstrative. The current prototype also contains illustrative sample data, and its media storage adapter simulates uploads rather than persisting files to a live Supabase Storage backend.

The prototype should therefore be evaluated as an early product concept, not as evidence that all proposed capabilities are operational.

### Current priorities

The next development stage is intended to focus on:

1. Persistent data storage and user accounts.
2. Functional organization and community groups.
3. Verified organizational roles and permission controls.
4. Persistent project posts, updates, and supporting documentation.
5. A basic partnership request and review workflow.
6. Appropriate privacy, moderation, and content verification safeguards.

## Proposed Technology Direction

The current prototype is built around web technologies, including HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Supabase has been explored as a potential foundation for backend services and data persistence. Completing the integration and defining the production architecture are part of the ongoing development work.

The final technology stack and deployment architecture may evolve as the MVP is developed.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Validate the Core Experience
Refine the prototype and gather feedback from nonprofit organizations, community leaders, volunteers, and potential collaborators.

### Phase 2 — Build a Functional MVP
Implement persistent organization groups, member roles, project updates, and basic moderation.

### Phase 3 — Introduce Partnerships
Develop structured collaboration requests, organization review, agreed objectives, and project follow-up.

### Phase 4 — Explore Resource Mobilization
Evaluate safe and transparent ways to support donations, material assistance, volunteering, and other forms of collaboration.

These phases describe the intended direction and are not commitments to specific delivery dates.

## Who Is Zuliov For?

- **Nonprofit organizations** seeking to communicate their activities and project progress.
- **Grassroots communities** looking for potential collaborators.
- **Volunteers and supporters** interested in discovering and following social initiatives.
- **Partner organizations** that can contribute resources, expertise, or outreach.

The initial goal is to validate the concept with a small number of organizations and communities before expanding to additional causes and regions.
